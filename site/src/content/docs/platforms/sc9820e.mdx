The SC9820E platform currently has one target: [Xiaomi Qin 1s+](/fplinux/phones/xiaomi-qin-1s-plus/). Its checklist records the supported functions and remaining limits. Use USB power; operation with a connected battery and charging are not verified.

## Display and images

The display supports native-size RGB565 images. [Image presentation](/fplinux/apps/image-tools/present/) provides `--mode cpu-rgb565` for NV16 input. Native NV16 presentation is not verified.

The UMS9117 ROTA and JPEG engines are separate implementations and do not apply to this platform. The rotation and JPEG applications are not available for SC9820E. [FFmpeg](/fplinux/apps/ffmpeg/) provides software decoding; it does not supply a video player or hardware video decoding.

## GPU rendering

The Mali-T820 MC1 supports [GPU rendering in ARMADA and TyrQuake](/fplinux/apps/#choose-a-renderer) through Panfrost and Mesa. Check the Qin page for application limits. The standard image starts the Weston/Wayland [phone interface](/fplinux/use/session/). GPU rendering, JPEG processing and video decoding are distinct functions.

## Audio and power

The [audio guide](/fplinux/use/audio/) provides the Qin speaker volume and built-in microphone commands. Playback and capture are mutually exclusive. Wired-headset routing is unsupported, and audio quality remains uncalibrated.

Manual CPU frequency selection, RTC alarms, suspend-to-idle, vibration and board-sensor readings have supported paths with limits recorded on the phone page. The board thermistor does not measure CPU temperature; automatic frequency scaling and CPU thermal protection are unavailable. Use the shared [power instructions](/fplinux/use/power/) for storage preparation before sleep or shutdown.

## Loading and storage

Builds and standalone archives for Linux and Windows include the RAM-loading stages. No separate FDL1, FDL2 or SML files are needed. Qin's first launch prepares its sound settings through the shared [phone-data workflow](/fplinux/guides/device-data/#prepare-data-from-the-phone). USB loading and SSH from Windows have not been verified with Qin 1s+.

The default system runs in RAM. The `microsd-root` profile loads Linux over USB and mounts the system root from an ext4 microSD card. Follow the shared [system-card instructions](/fplinux/guides/microsd-system-card/). Both profiles need the PC loader at every cold start. Qin exposes its internal eMMC user area only for reading; the [phone page](/fplinux/phones/xiaomi-qin-1s-plus/#storage-and-power) gives the supported use and limits.

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, FM, camera capture and mobile service are not supported in FPLinux on this platform. Preparing optional radio data does not enable them.

## Implemented functions

These labels describe shared platform implementations. Read the [Qin checklist](/fplinux/phones/xiaomi-qin-1s-plus/#support-checklist) for its hardware, stock-firmware functions, FPLinux results and limits. **Not implemented** does not establish absence of hardware, and a platform implementation does not establish support on every phone using that chip. A target can also supply its own board-specific implementation.
