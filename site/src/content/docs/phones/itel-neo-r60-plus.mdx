## Boot

Use the RAM profile and follow [Run FPLinux](/fplinux/start/run/) for the shared loader steps.

<PhoneBoot />

The 240×320 SPI display shows the graphical interface. The keypad has a 5×4 layout; its full keymap, brightness adjustment and display sleep/wake have not been tested. The USB loader leaves the display alone; screen output starts in Linux.

## Storage

The first-run flow has been verified on Windows. The launcher read a fresh raw NAND backup, prepared the phone data and reached the graphical interface on the next launch. The backup supplies this phone's audio profile, Bluetooth firmware and FM settings. See [Phone data](/fplinux/guides/device-data/) for saved backups and preparation.

A microSD data card inserted before startup mounted and appeared in Files. Card writes are untested. Keep the card installed while the phone is powered and [unmount it before shutdown](/fplinux/use/power/#unmount-data-cards). Hot-swap and a microSD system-root profile are unsupported.

## Audio and peripherals

The handset played an alarm, vibrated and lit its keypad during testing. Headphone playback, jack detection, Bluetooth and FM are configured and await testing on this handset. The phone's saved settings supply its playback gains and radio data.

Microphone capture and the camera are not configured. Hardware JPEG, image rotation and internal temperature reporting are configured but have not been tested.

## Clock and session limits

Setting the RTC has been observed over the authenticated USB shell. Time retention after power loss, alarm wake and system suspend have not been tested.
