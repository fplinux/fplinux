The configured panel is ST7789H2 at 240×320 over SPI. The keypad has a 5×4 layout. Physical display output, keypad input, brightness, and graphical applications have not been tested on this phone.

## Storage

The read-only raw NAND backup has been verified. FPLinux does not support mounting internal filesystems, writing internal storage, or restoring NAND. There is no microSD system profile; removable-card detection and data-card behavior are unverified.

## Clock and session limits

Setting the RTC has been observed over the authenticated USB shell. Time retention after power loss and alarm wake have not been tested. Audio, Bluetooth, camera, battery telemetry, removable-card hot-swap, and vibration are not enabled for this phone.
