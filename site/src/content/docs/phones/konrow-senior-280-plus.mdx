## Controls

The green call key is OK. The side Volume − key moves left and Volume + moves right. To unlock, press the left soft key and then `*`. The complete keymap still needs testing. The torch has its own physical switch.

## Storage and audio

Insert the microSD card before boot and [unmount it before shutdown](/fplinux/use/power/#unmount-data-cards). The card is available for files during the RAM session. The internal NAND has an identification diagnostic. Full NAND backup and automatic phone-data preparation are unavailable.

Wired headphones work with the default playback levels. Loudspeaker output, microphone input, automatic headset detection and Bluetooth are unavailable.

## Power

Disconnect the charger when checking [power-off](/fplinux/use/power/). The battery level is estimated from voltage and current readings.
