## Controls

The green call key is OK. The side Volume − key moves left and Volume + moves right. To unlock, press the left soft key and then `*`. The complete keymap still needs testing. The torch has its own physical switch.

## Storage and audio

Insert the microSD card before boot and [unmount it before shutdown](/fplinux/use/power/#unmount-data-cards). The card is available for files during the RAM session. The [first-run backup](/fplinux/start/run/) reads 136 MiB of raw NAND, including spare bytes. Keep the matching receipt alongside the backup when reusing it.

Wired headphones work. Phone-data preparation extracts their playback calibration from the NAND backup. Loudspeaker output, microphone input, automatic headset detection and Bluetooth are unavailable.

## Power

Disconnect the charger when checking [power-off](/fplinux/use/power/). The battery level is estimated from voltage and current readings.
